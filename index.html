Clear Answers & Collaboration

Questions to Ask Me (FAQ)

Key considerations, technical deliverables, and engineering workflow details to ensure smooth collaboration from day one.

Every project concludes with a complete, production-ready package tailored to your exact manufacturing and engineering requirements:

  • Native 3D CAD Files: Fully parametric models with editable feature trees (SolidWorks .sldprt, .sldasm or Autodesk Fusion 360).
  • Neutral 3D Exchange: High-precision standard formats including STEP (AP214 / AP242), IGES, and Parasolid (.x_t).
  • Additive & Prototyping Meshes: Clean, manifold .STL / .OBJ files optimized for 3D printing (SLA, SLS, FDM).
  • 2D Manufacturing Drawings: Complete fabrication drawings with dimensioning, Bill of Materials (BOM), title blocks, and notes (PDF, DWG, DXF).
  • Renders & Visuals: High-resolution photorealistic renderings for pitch decks, catalogs, and client presentations.

Seamless interoperability with your team's existing CAD infrastructure is guaranteed:

  • Primary Toolsets: SolidWorks, Autodesk Fusion 360, AutoCAD, KeyShot, and Blender.
  • Version Matching: We match your team's active SolidWorks or CAD release version so you can open native files directly without version conflicts or backwards-compatibility issues.
  • Cross-Platform Transfer: Flawless import/export workflows with Siemens NX, PTC Creo, CATIA, and Rhino using robust Parasolid and STEP AP242 protocols with intact assembly structures.

Precision is fundamental to mechanical engineering. All designs account for real-world mechanical fits and dimensional control:

  • GD&T Standards: Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing drafted in compliance with ASME Y14.5 or ISO GPS standards.
  • Fits & Clearances: Calculated standard ISO/ANSI fits (sliding, transition, press/interference fits) for shafts, bearings, pins, and custom mating components.
  • Interference & Clash Detection: 100% static and dynamic kinematic interference checks performed on every assembly to avoid physical collisions before machining.
  • Tolerance Stack-Up Analysis: 1D and 2D stack-up analysis on critical assembly chains to ensure predictable fits across batch manufacturing variations.

Every component is designed with strict Design for Manufacturing (DFM) and Design for Assembly (DFA) principles:

  • CNC Machining: Proper internal corner radii, standard tool reach considerations, uniform wall thickness, and minimized setup orientations.
  • Sheet Metal: Accurate bend deductions, K-factors, bend reliefs, and unfoldable flat patterns ready for laser cutting and press brake forming.
  • Injection Molding: Proper draft angles (1°–3°), uniform wall sections, rib-to-wall thickness ratios, parting lines, and avoidance of sink marks.
  • 3D Printing / Additive: Self-supporting geometries, print orientation optimization, and post-processing clearance allowances.
  • Material Recommendations: Practical selection guidance across metals (Aluminum 6061/7075, Stainless Steel 304/316, Brass) and engineering polymers (Delrin/POM, ABS, Nylon, Polycarbonate, PEEK) balancing strength, weight, and fabrication cost.

Predictable timelines, transparent pricing, and structured feedback ensure project success without surprises:

  • Turnaround Times: Fast execution tailored to your deadlines. Rapid prototypes can be delivered in as little as 24–48 hours; comprehensive assemblies follow clear milestone checkpoints.
  • Revision Rounds: Standard project proposals include 2 to 3 revision iterations to incorporate design refinements, assembly test feedback, or vendor tooling adjustments.
  • Flexible Engagement Models: Choose between fixed-price milestones for well-defined projects or hourly consulting for iterative R&D and continuous engineering support.
  • Transparent Estimates: You receive an upfront breakdown before work commences—no hidden fees or unexpected extras.
